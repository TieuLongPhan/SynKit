# Kappa causal-history comparison attribution

The comparison invokes the unmodified released KaSim and KaStor executables
from [KappaTools v4.1.3.1](https://github.com/Kappa-Dev/KappaTools/releases/tag/v4.1.3.1),
corresponding to commit `bafaeb8873cb4e4afd9b22ae312dc93724cfc2fa`.
Source headers identify CNRS, Harvard Medical School, INRIA and IRIF and
declare GNU LGPL version 3. The external checkout contains `LGPL-LICENSE`.
Archive and executable SHA256 values are pinned in the comparison protocol.

The implementation, binaries and primary-paper PDFs are not bundled in the
MTG package. The local adapter, Kappa model inputs and plots are original
work for this comparison. Native trace/model/story outputs are retained as
the evidence produced by the external tools, rather than copied source.

Cite the platform description and mathematical causal-compression foundation:

- [Boutillier et al., The Kappa platform for rule-based modeling (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty272).
- [Danos et al., Graphs, Rewriting and Pathway Reconstruction for Rule-Based Models (2012)](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2012.276).

The scope is formal anchored constructions. Native Kappa extraction/compression,
including weak and strong stories, is credited to those methods. No claim of
MTG superiority or a new general causal-compression theory follows.
