BioModels input notice
======================

The optional interoperability study downloads models from BioModels. The
BioModels dataset, including encoded models and annotations, is made available
under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication:

https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biomodels/termsofuse

Downloaded model files are cached locally under
``Experiment/CRN/data/biomodels`` but are excluded from SynKit distribution
artifacts. Evidence reports retain identifiers, source URLs, file sizes and
SHA-256 checksums.
