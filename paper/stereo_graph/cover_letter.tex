\begin{letter}{Editor-in-Chief\\Journal of Cheminformatics}
\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please consider the methodology article
\textbf{\textit{Exact canonicalization of configured molecular stereographs}}.
It addresses molecular identity when local configurations coexist with
whole-molecule symmetry, extended stereochemical supports, and incomplete
information. The manuscript separates configured identity from carrier
perception, derived nomenclature, and experimental configurational stability.

Ordered local frames, stereo-aware canonicalization, and rotation-based
coordination comparison are established by M\O D, Molassembler, RDKit, MACE,
and StereoMolGraph. Our advance is a uniform faithful reduction of a declared
catalogue of configured supports to a colored auxiliary graph, with explicit
transport, mirror, identity-profile, and information contracts. The
construction preserves and reflects stereo-isomorphism; full canonical
certificates then decide identity and mirror relations. Unresolved strict
inputs and computations that exceed their declared bounds refuse a
definitive result.

Validation uses an independent assignment-partition oracle on \IndependentCases{} fixtures
and \IndependentAssignments{} assignments, including simultaneous and coupled configurations.
All \MutationsRejected{} deliberate implementation defects are rejected. Newly sourced
coordination examples are annotated geometrically before production
evaluation: \ChemicalCoordinationCertified{} of \ChemicalCoordinationSelected{} satisfy the frozen fit criteria, and every certified
source/mirror and renumbering comparison agrees. The \ChemicalCoordinationUnresolved{} geometry refusals remain
in the selected denominator. A separate set of \ChemicalNegativeSelected{} sourced constitutional
negatives and a frozen, source-assisted review of \ChemicalRotASelected{} development-corpus
structural loci support explicitly bounded perception claims. These studies
do not establish experimental persistence. Controlled runtime and memory
measurements include resource-censored outcomes; a registration example
recovers independently specified groups but shows no speed advantage for
certificate grouping.

The article fits the journal's methodology scope through its representation,
proof, implementation, and reproducible evaluation. Machine-readable reports,
input checksums, independent fixtures, and maintained experiment runners
accompany the revision. The molecular identity result is self-contained;
reaction-rule transport lies outside the article's validation scope.

\closing{Sincerely,\\Tieu-Long Phan}
